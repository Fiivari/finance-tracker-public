Privacy policy

Oma talous is open-source software that each person runs for themselves. Every copy is operated by the account holder whose bank accounts it reads, for their own personal use only. There is no shared service and no operator in between.

What data is processed

Account information from the account holder's own bank, with their consent: account names and types, balances, and transactions (date, amount, counterparty name, message, bank transaction code). Account numbers (IBANs) are not stored; accounts are identified by a one-way hash.

Where it goes

  • The bank sends it to Enable Banking, a licensed account information service provider, under the account holder's own Enable Banking application.
  • From there it goes only to the account holder's own Google account: a Google Sheet and a Google Apps Script project that they own. Google processes it under the account holder's Google terms.
  • Nothing is sent to the authors of the software or to any other party.

Retention and control

The data stays in the account holder's Google Sheet until they delete it. Access is read-only and lasts at most 180 days per consent. It can be withdrawn at any time in the bank, in the Enable Banking control panel, or by deleting the Sheet and its script.

Contact

Each copy's operator is its account holder. Questions about the software: GitHub issues.
